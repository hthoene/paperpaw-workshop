| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[notes-vault] search.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.line` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[notes-vault] search.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
| `[notes-vault] search.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
