| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[kosync] user` | Account | text | `` | Sign in to continue reading on other devices, also in KOReader. |
| `[kosync] auto` | Sync automatically | `true`, `false` | `true` | Fetches the position when a book opens and sends it while reading. |
| `[kosync] server` | Server | text | `https://sync.koreader.rocks` | KOReader's sync server, or your own. Changing it signs you out. |
