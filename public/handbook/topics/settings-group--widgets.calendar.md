| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] calendar.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.center` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] calendar.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[widgets] calendar.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
| `[widgets] calendar.week-start` | First day of the week | `widgets.week-start.auto`, `widgets.week-start.monday`, `widgets.week-start.sunday` | `widgets.week-start.auto` | Which day each week of the calendar begins with. |
| `[widgets] calendar.mark` | Today marked by | `widgets.mark.circle`, `widgets.mark.square`, `widgets.mark.ring`, `widgets.mark.underline` | `widgets.mark.circle` | How today is marked in the month. |
| `[widgets] calendar.initials` | Weekdays' names | `widgets.initials.one`, `widgets.initials.two` | `widgets.initials.one` | How the weekdays above the month are named: by one letter or by two. |
| `[widgets] calendar.heading` | Month's name | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] calendar.year` | Year | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] calendar.weekdays` | Weekdays | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] calendar.weeks` | Week numbers | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] calendar.others` | Days of other months | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] calendar.weekends` | Weekends lighter | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] calendar.next` | The next month too | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
