| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[home] status.line` | Line | list | `` | What the status line shows: the items chosen below, or another status line. |
| `[home] status.left` | Left | list | `core.status.clock` | What the status line shows on this side. |
| `[home] status.center` | Centre | list | `` | What the status line shows on this side. |
| `[home] status.right` | Right | list | `lock.touch-status, core.status.battery` | What the status line shows on this side. |
| `[home] status.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[home] status.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
