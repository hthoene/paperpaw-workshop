| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[home] status.line` | Line | list | `` | What the status line shows: the items chosen for its parts below, or another status line. |
| `[home] status.left` | Left | list | `core.status.clock` | What the status line shows on this side. |
| `[home] status.center` | Centre | list | `` | What the status line shows on this side. |
| `[home] status.right` | Right | list | `lock.touch-status, core.status.battery` | What the status line shows on this side. |
| `[home] status.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in: nothing, a line round it, a light card or a dark card with light text. |
| `[home] status.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | How far what the widget shows keeps from its edges, or from its frame. Automatic suits most widgets. |
