| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] word-clock.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] word-clock.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[widgets] word-clock.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
| `[widgets] word-clock.style` | Style | `widgets.word-clock.style.text`, `widgets.word-clock.style.grid` | `widgets.word-clock.style.text` | Whether the time is written as text or picked out in a grid of letters. |
| `[widgets] word-clock.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large`, `core.size.fit` | `core.size.fit` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] word-clock.lead` | “It is” | `widgets.word-clock.lead.none`, `widgets.word-clock.lead.same`, `widgets.word-clock.lead.caption` | `widgets.word-clock.lead.same` | Whether “It is” begins the sentence, stands small above it, or is left out. |
| `[widgets] word-clock.quarters` | Quarters | `widgets.word-clock.quarters.past`, `widgets.word-clock.quarters.next` | `widgets.word-clock.quarters.past` | How quarters are said: past the hour, or towards the next one. |
| `[widgets] word-clock.capitals` | In capitals | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] word-clock.dots` | The minutes as dots | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
