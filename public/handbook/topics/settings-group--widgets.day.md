| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] day.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] day.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in: nothing, a line round it, a light card or a dark card with light text. |
| `[widgets] day.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | How far what the widget shows keeps from its edges, or from its frame. Automatic suits most widgets. |
| `[widgets] day.style` | Style | `widgets.day.style.weekday`, `widgets.day.style.number`, `widgets.day.style.sheet` | `widgets.day.style.weekday` | What is large: the weekday, the day's number, or a sheet of a tear-off calendar. |
| `[widgets] day.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large`, `core.size.fit` | `core.size.fit` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] day.date-format` | Date format | `widgets.date-format.long`, `widgets.date-format.weekday`, `widgets.date-format.numeric`, `widgets.date-format.iso` | `widgets.date-format.long` | How the date is written: in words, with the weekday, in numbers, or as year, month and day. |
| `[widgets] day.year` | Year | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] day.week` | Week number | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
