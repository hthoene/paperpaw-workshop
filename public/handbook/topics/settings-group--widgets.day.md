| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] day.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] day.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[widgets] day.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
| `[widgets] day.paper` | Colours | `core.plate.normal`, `core.plate.inverted`, `core.plate.light-gray`, `core.plate.dark-gray`, `core.plate.none` | `core.plate.normal` | Normal, inverted, a gray or transparent. Over a dark picture, pick light text. |
| `[widgets] day.ink` | Text | `core.ink.auto`, `core.tone.black`, `core.tone.white` | `core.ink.auto` | The colour of the widget's text; automatic is black or white, whichever reads. |
| `[widgets] day.corners` | Corners | `core.corners.auto`, `core.corners.square`, `core.corners.small`, `core.corners.round`, `core.corners.soft` | `core.corners.auto` | How round the widget's corners are; automatic follows the rest of the interface. |
| `[widgets] day.frame-line` | Frame line | `core.lines.auto`, `core.lines.thin`, `core.lines.medium`, `core.lines.thick` | `core.lines.auto` | How thick the line of the widget's frame is; automatic follows the interface's lines. |
| `[widgets] day.drop-shadow` | Shadow | `core.shadow.none`, `core.shadow.hard` | `core.shadow.none` | A solid shadow below and to the right of the widget's card or colour. |
| `[widgets] day.style` | Style | `widgets.day.style.weekday`, `widgets.day.style.number`, `widgets.day.style.sheet` | `widgets.day.style.weekday` | What is large: the weekday, the day's number, or a sheet of a tear-off calendar. |
| `[widgets] day.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large`, `core.size.fit` | `core.size.fit` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] day.date-format` | Date format | `widgets.date-format.long`, `widgets.date-format.weekday`, `widgets.date-format.numeric`, `widgets.date-format.iso` | `widgets.date-format.long` | How the date is written: in words or in numbers. |
| `[widgets] day.year` | Year | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] day.week` | Week number | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] day.font` | Font | `core.widgets.font.all`, `core.widgets.font.menus`, `core.widgets.font.book`, `PaperPaw Sans` | `core.widgets.font.all` | The font of this widget. Text too large for a font from the card stays in the menus' font. |
