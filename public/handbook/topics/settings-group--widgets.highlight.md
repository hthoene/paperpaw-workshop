| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] highlight.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.center` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] highlight.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[widgets] highlight.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
| `[widgets] highlight.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large`, `core.size.fit` | `core.size.fit` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] highlight.source` | Highlights from | `widgets.highlight.recent`, `widgets.highlight.all` | `widgets.highlight.recent` | Which books the highlight shown is taken from. |
| `[widgets] highlight.change` | Another one | `widgets.highlight.daily`, `widgets.highlight.shown` | `widgets.highlight.daily` | Each time it shows: a new one whenever the home screen shows again. |
| `[widgets] highlight.caption` | Heading | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] highlight.marks` | Large quotation mark | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] highlight.title` | Title | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] highlight.author` | Author | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] highlight.note` | Note | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
