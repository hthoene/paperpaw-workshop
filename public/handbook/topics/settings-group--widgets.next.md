| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] next.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] next.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[widgets] next.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
| `[widgets] next.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large` | `core.size.medium` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] next.pick` | Which book | `widgets.next.series`, `widgets.next.newest`, `widgets.next.random` | `widgets.next.series` | Without a next or new book, a random unread one is chosen. |
| `[widgets] next.caption` | Heading | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] next.cover` | Cover | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] next.author` | Author | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] next.series` | Series | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
