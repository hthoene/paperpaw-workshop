| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[core] hyphenate` | Hyphenate words | `true`, `false` | `true` | Break long words at the end of lines, in the book's language. |
| `[core] hyphen-min-left` | Letters before hyphen | 0 to 5 | `0` | The fewest letters left before a hyphen. 0: the language's own. |
| `[core] hyphen-min-right` | Letters after hyphen | 0 to 5 | `0` | The fewest letters carried after a hyphen. 0: the language's own. |
