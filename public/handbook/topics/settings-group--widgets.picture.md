| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] picture.folder` | Folder | text | `Pictures` | The folder on the card with the pictures. |
| `[widgets] picture.change` | Another one | `widgets.change.daily`, `widgets.change.shown` | `widgets.change.daily` | Each day: one a day, the same all day. Each time it shows: another whenever the home screen shows again, after a book or another screen. Choosing it always shows the next one. |
| `[widgets] picture.fit` | Picture | `widgets.picture.fit.fill`, `widgets.picture.fit.whole` | `widgets.picture.fit.fill` | Whether the picture fills its place, cut at its edges, or shows whole. |
| `[widgets] picture.caption` | Its name below | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] picture.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.center` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] picture.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in: nothing, a line round it, a light card or a dark card with light text. |
| `[widgets] picture.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | How far what the widget shows keeps from its edges, or from its frame. Automatic suits most widgets. |
