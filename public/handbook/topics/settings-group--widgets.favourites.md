| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] favourites.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.center` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] favourites.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[widgets] favourites.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
| `[widgets] favourites.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large` | `core.size.medium` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] favourites.layout` | Arrangement | `widgets.books.layout.row`, `widgets.books.layout.grid` | `widgets.books.layout.row` | Whether the favourite books stand in one row or in several. |
| `[widgets] favourites.count` | Books at most | 1 to 24 | `8` | How many books show at most; fewer where the place is small. |
| `[widgets] favourites.caption` | Heading | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] favourites.titles` | Titles | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] favourites.read` | Books read marked | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] favourites.current` | Book being read marked | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
