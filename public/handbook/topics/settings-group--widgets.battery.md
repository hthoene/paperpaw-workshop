| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] battery.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.center` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] battery.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in: nothing, a line round it, a light card or a dark card with light text. |
| `[widgets] battery.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | How far what the widget shows keeps from its edges, or from its frame. Automatic suits most widgets. |
| `[widgets] battery.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large`, `core.size.fit` | `core.size.fit` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] battery.style` | Style | `widgets.battery.lying`, `widgets.battery.standing`, `widgets.battery.ring` | `widgets.battery.lying` | How the battery is drawn: lying, standing, or as a ring round its charge. |
| `[widgets] battery.percent` | Percentage | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] battery.charging` | Charging | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] battery.caption` | Heading | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
