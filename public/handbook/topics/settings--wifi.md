| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[wifi] on-demand` | Connect when needed | `true`, `false` | `true` | Switches Wi-Fi on when needed and joins a saved network. |
| `[wifi] idle-off` | Switch off after | `wifi.idle-off.30s`, `wifi.idle-off.1m`, `wifi.idle-off.2m`, `wifi.idle-off.5m`, `wifi.idle-off.10m` | `wifi.idle-off.1m` | How long Wi-Fi stays on after its last use. |
| `[wifi] while-charging` | On while charging | `true`, `false` | `true` | Keeps Wi-Fi on in a saved network while the device is charging. |
| `[wifi] join-first` | Join first | `wifi.join-first.picked`, `wifi.join-first.strongest` | `wifi.join-first.picked` | Which saved network comes first when several are in reach. |
