| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] year-progress.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] year-progress.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[widgets] year-progress.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
| `[widgets] year-progress.style` | Style | `widgets.year.bar`, `widgets.year.ring`, `widgets.year.months` | `widgets.year.bar` | How the year so far shows: a bar, a ring, or a dot for each month. |
| `[widgets] year-progress.year` | Year | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] year-progress.percent` | Percent | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] year-progress.day` | Day of the year | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] year-progress.week` | Week number | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] year-progress.left` | Days left | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
