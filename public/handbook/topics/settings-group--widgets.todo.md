| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] todo.count` | Items shown | 1 to 12 | `5` | How many to-dos show at most. |
| `[widgets] todo.done` | Done | `widgets.todo.done.struck`, `widgets.todo.done.hidden` | `widgets.todo.done.struck` | What happens to a to-do once ticked off. |
| `[widgets] todo.box` | Boxes | `widgets.todo.box.circle`, `widgets.todo.box.square`, `widgets.todo.box.none` | `widgets.todo.box.circle` | The shape of the boxes to tick. |
| `[widgets] todo.caption` | Heading above | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] todo.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large` | `core.size.small` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] todo.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] todo.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[widgets] todo.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
