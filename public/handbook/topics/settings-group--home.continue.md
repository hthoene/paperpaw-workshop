| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[home] continue.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[home] continue.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.line` | What the widget is drawn in: nothing, a line round it, a light card or a dark card with light text. |
| `[home] continue.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | How far what the widget shows keeps from its edges, or from its frame. Automatic suits most widgets. |
| `[home] continue.cover` | Show the cover | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[home] continue.progress` | Show the progress | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
