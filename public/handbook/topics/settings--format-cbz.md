| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[format-cbz] direction` | Comic pages run | `format-cbz.direction.book`, `format-cbz.direction.ltr`, `format-cbz.direction.rtl` | `format-cbz.direction.book` | Manga: the page turns are mirrored. |
| `[format-cbz] panels` | Panel by panel | `true`, `false` | `false` | One panel at a time, page turns step through them. |
