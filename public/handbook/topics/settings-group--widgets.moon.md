| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] moon.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] moon.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in: nothing, a line round it, a light card or a dark card with light text. |
| `[widgets] moon.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | How far what the widget shows keeps from its edges, or from its frame. Automatic suits most widgets. |
| `[widgets] moon.name` | Phase's name | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] moon.full` | Next full moon | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] moon.lit` | How much is lit | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] moon.shadow` | Shadow | `widgets.moon.shadow.dark`, `widgets.moon.shadow.light` | `widgets.moon.shadow.dark` | Whether the moon's unlit part is drawn dark or light. |
| `[widgets] moon.hemisphere` | Seen from | `widgets.moon.north`, `widgets.moon.south` | `widgets.moon.north` | Where the moon is seen from: in the south it looks mirrored. |
| `[widgets] moon.date-format` | Date format | `widgets.date-format.long`, `widgets.date-format.weekday`, `widgets.date-format.numeric`, `widgets.date-format.iso` | `widgets.date-format.long` | How the date is written: in words, with the weekday, in numbers, or as year, month and day. |
