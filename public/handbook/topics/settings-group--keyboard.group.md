| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[keyboard] layouts` | Keyboard layouts | list | `keyboard.layout.auto` | The layouts to type with; a key switches between them. |
| `[keyboard] umlaut-keys` | Umlaut keys | `true`, `false` | `false` | Keys of their own for ä, ö, ü and ß. Off, hold a, o, u or s for them. |
| `[keyboard] key-shape` | Key shape | `keyboard.key-shape.round`, `keyboard.key-shape.tall`, `keyboard.key-shape.wide` | `keyboard.key-shape.round` | Taller keys make the keyboard higher, wider keys make it lower. |
| `[keyboard] size` | Size | `keyboard.size.small`, `keyboard.size.medium`, `keyboard.size.large` | `keyboard.size.small` | Smaller shows more of the page. Larger keys and letters are easier to hit and read. |
| `[keyboard] auto-capitals` | Automatic capitals | `true`, `false` | `false` | A capital at the start and after . ! or ?. Not in passwords and addresses. |
