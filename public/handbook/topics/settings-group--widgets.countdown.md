| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] countdown.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] countdown.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in; a dark card has light text. |
| `[widgets] countdown.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | Space between the widget's content and its edges or frame. |
| `[widgets] countdown.title` | Title | text | `` | What happens that day, e.g. “Holiday”; without a title, the date shows. |
| `[widgets] countdown.date` | Date | text | `12-24` | 2026-12-24 for a day once, 12-24 for each year (e.g. a birthday). |
| `[widgets] countdown.style` | Style | `widgets.countdown.style.number`, `widgets.countdown.style.sentence` | `widgets.countdown.style.number` | Whether the days left show as a large number or in a sentence. |
| `[widgets] countdown.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large`, `core.size.fit` | `core.size.fit` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] countdown.show-date` | Date | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] countdown.weeks` | In weeks too | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] countdown.date-format` | Date format | `widgets.date-format.long`, `widgets.date-format.weekday`, `widgets.date-format.numeric`, `widgets.date-format.iso` | `widgets.date-format.weekday` | How the date is written: in words or in numbers. |
