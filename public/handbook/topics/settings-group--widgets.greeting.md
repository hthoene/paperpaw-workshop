| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] greeting.name` | Your name | text | `` | Greeted by it: Good evening, Anna. |
| `[widgets] greeting.line` | Below | `widgets.greeting.line.none`, `widgets.greeting.line.date` | `widgets.greeting.line.date` | What shows below the greeting. |
| `[widgets] greeting.icon` | Sun or moon before it | `true`, `false` | `false` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] greeting.face` | Font | `widgets.face.ui`, `widgets.face.book`, `widgets.face.italic` | `widgets.face.ui` | The font the text is written in. |
| `[widgets] greeting.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large`, `core.size.fit` | `core.size.fit` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] greeting.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.center` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] greeting.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in: nothing, a line round it, a light card or a dark card with light text. |
| `[widgets] greeting.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | How far what the widget shows keeps from its edges, or from its frame. Automatic suits most widgets. |
