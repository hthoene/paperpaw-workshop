| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[reading-goal] content` | Content from | 5 to 95 | `50` | From which share of the goal the cat is content rather than meh. |
| `[reading-goal] happy` | Happy from | 50 to 200 | `100` | From which share of the goal the cat is happy. 100 % is the goal itself. |
| `[reading-goal] pictures` | Own pictures | text | `` | A folder with pictures named sad, meh, content and happy (PNG or JPEG) to show instead of the cats. Empty: the cats. |
| `[reading-goal] widget.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in: nothing, a line round it, a light card or a dark card with light text. |
| `[reading-goal] widget.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | How far what the widget shows keeps from its edges, or from its frame. Automatic suits most widgets. |
| `[reading-goal] widget.minutes` | Minutes read | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
