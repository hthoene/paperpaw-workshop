| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[opds] covers` | Catalog covers | `true`, `false` | `true` | Pictures of the books in the lists of catalogs, on devices with the memory for them. |
| `[opds] sync-limit` | Books per sync | 10 to 1000 | `50` | The most books a catalog gets in one sync. |
