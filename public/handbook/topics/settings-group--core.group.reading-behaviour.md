| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[core] reading-app` | Reading app | `core.reading-app.default` | `core.reading-app.default` | The app books open in; formats, books and folders can have their own. |
| `[core] footnotes` | Footnotes | `core.footnotes.popup`, `core.footnotes.jump` | `core.footnotes.popup` | A tapped note mark (1, [2], *) shows its note over the page, or goes to it. |
| `[format-cbz] direction` | Comic pages run | `format-cbz.direction.book`, `format-cbz.direction.ltr`, `format-cbz.direction.rtl` | `format-cbz.direction.book` | Manga: the page turns are mirrored. |
| `[format-cbz] panels` | Panel by panel | `true`, `false` | `false` | One panel at a time, page turns step through them. |
| `[reader] open-last` | Open last book at start | `true`, `false` | `true` | Start with the last book instead of the home screen. |
| `[reader] turn-wide-pictures` | Turn wide pictures | `true`, `false` | `false` | On: a wide picture shown full screen is turned to fill the screen. |
| `[reader] auto-turn-seconds` | Turn pages every | 5 to 600 | `30` | For “Turn pages automatically” in the reader’s menu. |
