| Setting | Name | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[widgets] reading.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |
| `[widgets] reading.frame` | Frame | `core.frame.none`, `core.frame.line`, `core.frame.card`, `core.frame.dark` | `core.frame.none` | What the widget is drawn in: nothing, a line round it, a light card or a dark card with light text. |
| `[widgets] reading.padding` | Spacing | `core.padding.auto`, `core.padding.none`, `core.padding.small`, `core.padding.normal`, `core.padding.large` | `core.padding.auto` | How far what the widget shows keeps from its edges, or from its frame. Automatic suits most widgets. |
| `[widgets] reading.size` | Size | `core.size.small`, `core.size.medium`, `core.size.large` | `core.size.medium` | How large the widget's text is. Where it does not fit, it is made smaller. |
| `[widgets] reading.caption` | Heading | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] reading.cover` | Cover | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] reading.author` | Author | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] reading.pages` | Page and percentage | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] reading.bar` | Progress bar | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] reading.left` | Time left to read | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] reading.chapter` | Chapter | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
| `[widgets] reading.chapter-left` | Pages left in the chapter | `true`, `false` | `true` | Whether the widget shows this. Where there is too little room, it may be left out anyway. |
