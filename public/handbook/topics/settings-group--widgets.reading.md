| `[widgets] reading.align` | Alignment | `core.align.left`, `core.align.center`, `core.align.right` | `core.align.left` | Where the widget's text sits in its place: at the left, in the middle or at the right. |

| `[widgets] reading.ink` | Text | `core.ink.auto`, `core.tone.black`, `core.tone.white` | `core.ink.auto` | The colour of the widget's text; automatic is black or white, whichever reads. |

| `[widgets] reading.frame-line` | Frame line | `core.lines.auto`, `core.lines.thin`, `core.lines.medium`, `core.lines.thick` | `core.lines.auto` | How thick the line of the widget's frame is; automatic follows the interface's lines. |
| `[widgets] reading.drop-shadow` | Shadow | `core.shadow.none`, `core.shadow.hard` | `core.shadow.none` | A solid shadow below and to the right of the widget's card or colour. |
